How to take your measurements

Use a soft measuring tape, keep it level, and measure over light clothing. The tape should sit comfortably against your body without pulling tight.

↕

Height

Stand barefoot on a hard, level floor with your back against a wall.

  1. Stand straight with your heels against the wall.
  2. Look straight ahead and ask someone to mark the top of your head.
  3. Measure from the floor to the mark.
◯

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, usually across the nipple line.

  1. Wrap the tape under your arms and across your back.
  2. Keep the tape level all the way around.
  3. Relax your arms and breathe normally while you read it.
⌁

Shoulder

Measure across the back from one shoulder point to the other.

  1. Find the bony tip at the outer edge of each shoulder.
  2. Measure in a straight line between those points across your back.
  3. Keep your shoulders relaxed and level.
◎

Waist

Measure around your waist where your trousers normally sit.

  1. Stand naturally without pulling in your stomach.
  2. Wrap the tape around your waist, keeping it parallel to the floor.
  3. Leave enough room to slide a finger under the tape.
◉

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.

  1. Stand with your feet together and weight evenly distributed.
  2. Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your hips and buttocks.
  3. Check in a mirror that the tape stays level.
↘

Inseam

This is the inside-leg length, from the crotch to the ankle.

  1. Stand straight with your feet slightly apart.
  2. Measure from the top of the inside leg down to your ankle bone.
  3. For easier measuring, use a well-fitting pair of trousers laid flat.
Quick tip

Measurements can vary slightly from day to day. For the most consistent result, use the same tape and measuring method each time.
